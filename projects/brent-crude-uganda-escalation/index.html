Working paper

Brent Crude Uganda Escalation

Construction Economics Construction Law
Code and data on GitHub ↗

The code, data and full documentation are on GitHub. Last updated 2026-11-20.

The question

When world oil prices jump, how much of the shock reaches Ugandan construction costs, and through which channels?

What's inside
  • Transmission models from Brent crude to exchange rates, consumer prices, diesel and construction input prices
  • Validation against an anonymised contract's price-adjustment history
  • A monthly forecast-calibration track
Why it matters

Price-escalation clauses are meant to share oil-price risk fairly; this tests whether they track what really happens to costs.

This is a working paper: the analysis is in progress and findings may change. The paper is not yet public; the code and data are on GitHub. Get in touch to discuss the work.
