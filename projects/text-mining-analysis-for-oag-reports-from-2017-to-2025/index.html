Text Mining Analysis For OAG Reports From 2017 To 2025

Construction Law Machine Learning
Code and data on GitHub ↗

The code, data and full documentation are on GitHub. Last updated 2026-11-10.

The question

What do Uganda's Auditor General reports reveal about what drives disputes on public infrastructure projects?

What's inside
  • Collection and text extraction of the Auditor General's annual reports, financial years 2017/18 to 2024/25
  • Identification and classification of passages describing dispute drivers
  • A comparison of text classifiers on the resulting corpus
Why it matters

Audit reports hold years of evidence on why projects go wrong; reading them at scale turns that evidence into patterns that can guide prevention.
