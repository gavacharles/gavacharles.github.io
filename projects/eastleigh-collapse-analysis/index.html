Eastleigh Collapse Analysis

Urban Planning Deep Learning Remote Sensing
Code and data on GitHub ↗

The code, data and full documentation are on GitHub. Last updated 2026-11-10.

The question

What can public evidence tell us about why a building on 9th Street, Eastleigh, Nairobi collapsed in October 2026?

What's inside
  • Video kinematics, physics-based models and physics-informed neural networks
  • A structural digital twin, GIS, rainfall, soils and remote-sensing evidence
  • A Bayesian ranking of candidate causes, with stated limits and recommended investigations
Why it matters

It shows how structural engineering and open data can guide an investigation quickly. It is a preliminary analysis, not a determination of cause or fault.
