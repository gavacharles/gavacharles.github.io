Kampala Emobility Atlas

Urban Planning Remote Sensing Cartography
Open the atlas ↗Code and data on GitHub ↗

The code, data and full documentation are on GitHub. Last updated 2026-11-20.

The question

Where in Greater Kampala are electric bodas and cars most viable, and where should battery-swap and fast-charging stations go?

What's inside
  • Suitability scoring for electric bodas and cars across the city
  • Optimised siting of battery-swap and fast-charger stations, with GIS and satellite screening
  • Economics and equity analysis, published as an interactive atlas
Why it matters

It helps operators, investors and the city plan charging networks that serve riders fairly and speed the shift to cleaner transport.
