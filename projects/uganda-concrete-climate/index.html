Working paper

Uganda Concrete Climate

Climate Change
Code and data on GitHub ↗

The code, data and full documentation are on GitHub. Last updated 2026-11-20.

The question

How will a changing climate affect corrosion in Uganda's reinforced-concrete buildings, and which buildings are most exposed?

What's inside
  • Climate projections combined with a probabilistic carbonation model and building-age data
  • Machine learning on map data to estimate building heights and exposure, plus urban heat and rainfall extremes
  • Two companion papers, one on corrosion risk and one on exposure of the building stock
Why it matters

Rising temperatures and CO₂ can shorten the life of concrete structures; knowing where risk concentrates guides inspection and design codes.

This is a working paper: the analysis is in progress and findings may change. The paper is not yet public; the code and data are on GitHub. Get in touch to discuss the work.
