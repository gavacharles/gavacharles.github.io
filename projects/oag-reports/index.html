The question

Where in Uganda do infrastructure disputes cluster, and how do audit findings compare with what the media reports?

What's inside
  • Text mining, topic models and classifiers applied to Auditor General reports from 2017 to 2025
  • A spatial analysis of where disputes occur, including geographically weighted regression and space–time patterns
  • A comparison of audit and media discourse on infrastructure disputes
Why it matters

Knowing where and why disputes concentrate helps government target supervision, capacity building and dispute-avoidance measures.

This is a working paper: the analysis and draft are in progress and findings may change. Please cite as a working paper.
