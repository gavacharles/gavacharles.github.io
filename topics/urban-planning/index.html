Urban Planning

Notes on cities, land use, and urban design.

Last updated 2026-11-20

  • Smell Pittsburgh Viewer: Residents' odour reports mapped to track air pollution across Pittsburgh; reports go daily to the county health department. air-quality citizen-science — main entry in Cartography
  • WorldPop API v3: Draw an area on a map and get how many people live there, by age and sex, on a free 100 m grid for 2015–2030 (per the post; the API page itself has no docs). population worldpop demographics gridded-data api — see Data
  • Open City · Smell, Cluj-Napoca: 3D map of which neighbourhoods sit downwind of a landfill in winter. landfill environmental-nuisance — main entry in Cartography
  • Modeling the spatial dynamics of income in cities (arXiv): Verbavatz & Barthelemy show a neighbourhood's income change depends strongly on its neighbours, across 45 US and 9 French urban areas; a simple voter model from statistical physics (1–2 parameters) predicts it, e.g. 2030 odds of income-status change in Boston and Paris. Shared by Milan Janosov. income-dynamics segregation voter-model neighbourhoods statistical-physics — see Machine Learning
  • Kileleshwa: 8 to 87 towers: Jesse Magal on how plot-by-plot approvals took Nairobi's Kileleshwa from ~8 to ~87 towers in 14 years, on 1948 infrastructure sized for 151 homes. Drivers: 2004 rezoning, sixfold land prices, a 2020 law easing sale of individual flats; asks whether the new 30-floor policy can fix cumulative infrastructure strain. densification zoning nairobi infrastructure-capacity cumulative-impact
