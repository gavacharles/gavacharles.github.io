Curriculum vitae

Charles Gavamukulya

A civil engineer and construction law practitioner with over six years of experience across the roads, rail, aviation, energy and gas, telecommunications, buildings and water sectors in Uganda. Practice is concentrated on contract administration, the preparation and determination of claims for extension of time and additional payment under FIDIC and bespoke forms of contract, and the resolution of construction disputes through mediation, adjudication and arbitration. Currently reading for a PhD in Construction Management at the University of Johannesburg on a dispute management framework for the Ugandan construction industry.
